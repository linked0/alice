# 119 · Line — That would usually help take my mind off the hunger.
title_ko: That would usually help take my mind off the hunger. — 그러면 보통 배고픈 걸 잠시 잊는 데 도움이 됐다
situation: Jay has a health check-up at a clinic this afternoon and has not eaten since last night. Over the office kettle, Aoife notices he skipped breakfast and asks how he copes. Jay recalls what he used to do on fasting mornings.
situation_ko: Jay는 오늘 오후 클리닉에서 건강검진이 있어 어젯밤부터 아무것도 먹지 않았다. 사무실 주전자 앞에서 Aoife가 아침을 거른 것을 알아채고 어떻게 버티느냐고 묻는다. Jay는 금식하는 아침마다 하던 일을 떠올린다.
why: **Take my mind off the hunger** means to redirect attention away from something that keeps nagging — hunger, a pending offer, a flaky test — without solving it. The object can be anything that occupies your head: take your mind off work, off the interview, off things. The **would** in "that would usually help" is not conditional; it is the habitual would of remembered routine, "that used to help", so nothing hypothetical is being claimed. Do not read it as "would help if…", and note that "help take" drops "to" and "me" naturally in speech.

## Dialogue
Aoife: No breakfast? You look like you're staring at that kettle.
> 아침 안 먹었어? 주전자를 노려보고 있는 것 같은데.
Jay: Health check at two, so nothing since last night. It's fine — I've done this before.
> 두 시에 건강검진이라 어젯밤부터 아무것도 안 먹었어. 괜찮아, 전에도 해 봤어.
Aoife: How did you get through the morning?
> 아침 시간은 어떻게 버텼어?
Jay: I'd pick the most boring ticket and go deep on it. That would usually help take my mind off the hunger.
> 제일 지루한 티켓을 골라서 거기 깊이 빠지곤 했어. 그러면 보통 배고픈 걸 잠시 잊는 데 도움이 됐거든.
Aoife: "Would help" — so it worked, or it would work?
> "would help"라니, 실제로 됐다는 거야, 될 거라는 거야?
Jay: It did. That "would" just means "used to". Fasting mornings, boring tickets, every time.
> 됐어. 그 "would"는 그냥 "~하곤 했다"야. 금식하는 아침엔 지루한 티켓, 매번 그랬어.

## Techniques
1. **과거의 습관은 would로 회상하고, 되물으면 "used to"로 풀어 준다.** "That would usually help…" → "That 'would' just means 'used to'." — 가정법으로 오해받을 때 한 줄로 정리한다.
2. **해결이 아니라 주의 전환임을 동사로 표시한다.** "take my mind off the hunger" — 배고픔을 없앤 게 아니라 거기서 신경을 돌렸다는 뜻이라 과장이 되지 않는다.
