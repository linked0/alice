situation: A recruiter, Nora, calls Jay for a reference on a former teammate who is interviewing abroad. She has the technical side covered and asks what he was like to work beside, so Jay has to describe a personality in two short sentences.

why: **He is easy to get on with** is the British form of "easy to get along with" — no friction day to day. **A good mixer** is someone who joins new groups easily; it sounds slightly old-fashioned but is still standard in a reference. Two traps: "get on with something" means carry on with a task, a different verb entirely, and a mixer here is a person, not a machine or a drink.

## Dialogue
Nora: Skills aside, what was he like to sit next to for a year?
> 기술은 빼고요, 일 년 동안 옆자리에 앉아 보니 어떤 사람이던가요?
Jay: He is easy to get on with. He's a good mixer, so he settled into the team in about a week.
> 어울리기 쉬운 사람이에요. 사람들 속에 잘 섞여서 한 주 만에 팀에 자리를 잡았고요.
Nora: A good mixer. I should write that one down.
> a good mixer라. 받아 적어야겠네요.
Jay: It means he walks into a room of strangers and comes out with two friends. Useful on a team that hires every month.
> 낯선 사람들만 있는 방에 들어가서 친구 둘을 만들어 나온다는 뜻이에요. 매달 채용하는 팀에는 쓸모 있는 자질이죠.
Nora: And under pressure?
> 압박이 있을 때는요?
Jay: The same person. He asked for help early, which is why nothing ever got stuck on him.
> 똑같습니다. 일찍 도움을 청하는 사람이라 그 사람한테서 막히는 일이 없었어요.

## Techniques
1. **인성 질문에는 한 문장 평가 + 한 문장 근거로 답한다.** "He's a good mixer, so he settled into the team in about a week." 평가 뒤에 관찰 가능한 사실을 붙이면 빈말로 들리지 않는다.
2. **상대가 모르는 표현을 쓰면 바로 그림으로 풀어 준다.** "walks into a room of strangers and comes out with two friends" 같은 설명이 사전적 정의보다 오래 남는다.
