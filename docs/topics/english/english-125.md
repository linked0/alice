why: **In limbo** is an undecided, suspended state where you can neither move on nor settle; **keep someone in limbo** is to leave them there by withholding an answer. The image comes from Catholic theology, the place that is neither heaven nor hell. The nearest everyday phrase is **string someone along**, which adds the sense of false hope. The drama line "I'll have my fun with her and keep her in limbo" is a cynical man talking about a woman, and "have my fun with her" is not something Jay should say about anyone; the piece to keep is "keep her in limbo", which fits a recruiter, a vendor, or a decision that keeps slipping.

## Dialogue
Yusuf: Any word from the Berlin team?
> 베를린 팀에서 연락 왔어?
Jay: Nothing real. Three weeks of "we'll be in touch". They're keeping me in limbo — I can't accept the local offer, and I can't plan around theirs.
> 제대로 된 건 없어. 3주째 "연락드리겠다"뿐이야. 나를 계속 붕 뜬 상태로 두고 있어. 국내 오퍼를 받을 수도 없고, 그쪽 걸 전제로 계획을 세울 수도 없고.
Yusuf: Maybe they're just slow.
> 그냥 느린 걸 수도 있잖아.
Jay: Maybe. But if they're stringing me along while they wait on another candidate, I'd rather know. I'll give them a date.
> 그럴 수도. 근데 다른 후보 기다리면서 나한테 희망만 주는 거라면 차라리 아는 게 낫지. 날짜를 줄 거야.
Yusuf: A date?
> 날짜?
Jay: "I have another offer expiring Friday. Can you tell me where I stand by Thursday?" Either way, I'm out of limbo.
> "금요일에 만료되는 다른 오퍼가 있습니다. 목요일까지 제 상황을 알려 주실 수 있을까요?" 어느 쪽이든 붕 뜬 상태에서는 벗어나지.

## Techniques
1. **상태를 이름 붙이고, 그 상태의 비용을 두 절로 말한다.** "They're keeping me in limbo — I can't accept…, and I can't plan around…" — 왜 애매함이 문제인지가 바로 드러난다.
2. **애매한 상태는 기한을 제시해 끝낸다.** "Can you tell me where I stand by Thursday?" — 상대를 비난하지 않고 답을 강제하는 문장이다.
