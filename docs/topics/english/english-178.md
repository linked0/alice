# 178 · Line — I'd rather we didn't agree yet.
title_ko: I'd rather we didn't agree yet. — 우리 아직은 합의하지 않는 편이 좋겠어요
situation: An architecture meeting. After ten minutes the room is converging on adopting a third-party workflow engine. Jay thinks it is probably reversible but wants a one-week spike before the team commits, and has to ask a group of peers to hold off without sounding like he is blocking.
situation_ko: 아키텍처 회의. 10분 만에 회의실이 외부 워크플로 엔진 도입으로 모이고 있다. Jay는 되돌릴 수 있는 결정일 거라 보지만 팀이 확정하기 전에 1주짜리 스파이크를 원하고, 막는 것처럼 들리지 않게 동료들에게 보류를 요청해야 한다.
why: **I'd rather we didn't agree yet** — when the subject after "would rather" is someone other than the speaker (we, you, they), the verb goes into the past subjunctive: "I'd rather we didn't", "I'd rather you waited". The trap is "I'd rather we not agree", which sounds off. When the subject is yourself, use "I'd rather not + verb": "I'd rather not decide today". In a meeting the plainer form is "Let's hold off on deciding just yet." The move behind the grammar is deferring consensus to the last responsible moment, when a cheap test can replace a guess.
why_ko: **I'd rather we didn't agree yet** — "would rather" 뒤의 주어가 화자가 아닌 다른 사람(we, you, they)이면 동사는 과거 가정법이 된다. "I'd rather we didn't", "I'd rather you waited". 함정은 "I'd rather we not agree"로, 어색하게 들린다. 주어가 나 자신이면 "I'd rather not + 동사"를 쓴다. "I'd rather not decide today". 회의에서 더 평이한 형태는 "Let's hold off on deciding just yet."이다. 이 문법 뒤의 움직임은 싼 실험이 추측을 대신할 수 있는 마지막 책임 시점까지 합의를 미루는 것이다.

## Techniques
1. **보류를 요청할 때는 기간과 결과물을 함께 말한다.** "I'd rather we didn't agree yet. Give me a one-week spike and I'll bring numbers instead of opinions." — 막는 게 아니라 조건부 연기가 된다.
2. **"would rather + 다른 주어"는 과거형.** "I'd rather we didn't agree", "I'd rather you waited". 주어가 나면 "I'd rather not decide".
