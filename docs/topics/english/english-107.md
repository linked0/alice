# 107 · Line — I'm not gonna lie. I thought you'd be gone longer than a week.
title_ko: I'm not gonna lie. I thought you'd be gone longer than a week. — 솔직히, 일주일 넘게 자리를 비울 줄 알았어
situation: Sam comes back to the office after what was supposed to be a two-week trip home to Dublin and shows up on Monday after only seven days. Jay, at the coffee machine, wants to say he is surprised without making it sound like a complaint.
situation_ko: Sam이 더블린 본가에 2주 다녀오기로 했는데 7일 만인 월요일에 출근한다. Jay는 커피 머신 앞에서 놀랐다는 말을 하되 불평처럼 들리지 않게 하고 싶다.
why: **I'm not gonna lie** is the modern spoken opener for 솔직히 말하면, closer to "honestly" than to a real promise not to lie; in writing use "honestly". **Be gone** describes the state of being away; "you'd" is "you would", the past-tense guess. The trap is treating "gone" as "left for good": "She's gone to Osaka" only means she is away.

## Dialogue
Sam: Morning. Yes, I know, I'm back early.
> 좋은 아침. 그래, 알아, 일찍 돌아왔지.
Jay: I'm not gonna lie. I thought you'd be gone longer than a week. Did Dublin kick you out?
> 솔직히, 일주일 넘게 자리를 비울 줄 알았어. 더블린이 널 쫓아냈어?
Sam: My mum's knee surgery got moved up, so I flew back once she was home.
> 어머니 무릎 수술이 앞당겨져서, 집에 돌아오시자마자 비행기를 탔어.
Jay: Fair enough. For the record, nobody was counting the days.
> 그렇구나. 말해 두자면 아무도 날짜를 세고 있진 않았어.
Sam: Good, because I'm still on Irish time.
> 다행이다, 아직 아일랜드 시간에 맞춰져 있거든.
Jay: Then take the morning. I'll be gone at two for the dentist; ping me before then.
> 그럼 오전은 쉬어. 난 두 시에 치과 가느라 자리를 비우니까 그 전에 불러.

## Techniques
1. **놀람은 "not gonna lie"로 열고, 바로 농담이나 배려로 닫는다.** "I'm not gonna lie. I thought you'd be gone longer" 뒤에 "Did Dublin kick you out?"을 붙여 불평이 아니라 반가움임을 보인다.
2. **"be gone"은 상태다.** "you'd be gone longer than a week", "I'll be gone at two" — 떠나는 동작이 아니라 자리를 비운 기간을 말할 때 쓴다.

## Expressions
| Expression | 뜻 · 쓰이는 자리 |
|---|---|
| I'm not gonna lie | 솔직히 말하면 — 회화의 도입구, 글에서는 honestly. "I'm not gonna lie. I thought…" |
| be gone (for a week) | 자리를 비우다, 떠나 있다 — 기간과 함께. "I'll be gone at two" |
| for the record | 분명히 해 두자면 — 오해를 막을 때. "For the record, nobody was counting the days." |
