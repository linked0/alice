# 134 · Word — two-time
title_ko: two-time — (애인·배우자를) 속이고 바람피우다
situation: Over drinks after work, Sam tells Jay that a mutual friend just found out her boyfriend had a second girlfriend in another city. Jay wants the plain verb for that, and not to confuse it with "two times"; he also wants to know whether the word is one people still say.
situation_ko: 퇴근 후 술자리에서 Sam이 Jay에게, 둘 다 아는 친구가 남자친구에게 다른 도시에 또 다른 여자친구가 있었다는 걸 방금 알았다고 말한다. Jay는 그걸 가리키는 평범한 동사를 쓰고 싶고, "two times"와 헷갈리고 싶지 않다. 요즘도 쓰는 말인지도 알고 싶다.
why: **Two-time** is a verb: to two-time someone is to cheat on a partner by secretly seeing someone else, running two relationships at once. It is informal and a little old-fashioned, common in songs and gossip, and it is always the cheater who two-times the partner ("He two-timed her"), never the reverse. The neutral modern phrase is **cheat on someone**. Do not confuse it with "two times" (twice) or the adjective "two-time" as in "a two-time champion", which is a compliment.
why_ko: **two-time**은 동사다. to two-time someone은 몰래 다른 사람을 만나며 애인을 속이다, 즉 두 관계를 동시에 굴리는 것이다. 비격식이고 약간 옛스러워서 노래 가사나 뒷담화에 흔하고, 언제나 바람피우는 쪽이 주어다("He two-timed her"). 중립적인 현대 표현은 **cheat on someone**이다. "two times"(두 번)나 "a two-time champion"(2회 우승자)처럼 칭찬인 형용사 two-time과 혼동하면 안 된다.

## Dialogue
Sam: Did you hear about Jiwoo? Turns out her boyfriend had a whole other girlfriend in Busan.
> 지우 얘기 들었어? 알고 보니 남자친구한테 부산에 완전히 다른 여자친구가 있었대.
Jay: So he was two-timing her. For how long?
> 그러니까 걔가 지우를 two-timing했다는 거네. 얼마나 오래?
Sam: Two-timing? Is that like "two times"?
> two-timing? "두 번" 같은 뜻이야?
Jay: No, it's a verb. To two-time someone is to cheat on them by secretly seeing someone else, two relationships at once. It sounds a bit old-school; most people would just say he cheated on her.
> 아니, 동사야. to two-time someone은 몰래 다른 사람을 만나면서 애인을 속이는 거야, 관계 두 개를 동시에. 약간 옛날 말투이긴 해서, 요즘은 그냥 he cheated on her라고 하지.
Sam: Old-school or not, it fits. About a year, apparently.
> 옛날 말이든 아니든 딱 맞네. 1년 정도였대.
Jay: A year of two-timing her. She's better off knowing.
> 1년을 two-timing했다니. 알게 된 게 지우한테 낫지.

## Techniques
1. **되물음에 뜻만이 아니라 격(register)까지 답한다.** "It sounds a bit old-school; most people would just say he cheated on her." — 단어를 알려 주면서 실제로 더 흔한 표현도 같이 준다.
2. **동사의 주어 방향을 문장으로 고정한다.** "he was two-timing her" — 항상 바람피운 쪽이 주어, 속은 쪽이 목적어. 이 순서를 그대로 외운다.
