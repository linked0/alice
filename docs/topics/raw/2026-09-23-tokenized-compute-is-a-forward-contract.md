# Source: BlackRock — "The Machine-Native Economy" (2026-09-22)

jay pasted one line on 2026-09-23: "JUST IN: BlackRock says AI compute could be tokenized in the
future." No URL came with it, so the claims below were reopened from secondary coverage; the white
paper itself was not read directly and should be, before anything in the card is quoted as BlackRock's
own wording.

## What the coverage reports

- Publisher: BlackRock Digital Assets Research team.
- Title: "The Machine-Native Economy: How digital assets connect intelligence, commerce, and compute".
- Date: 2026-09-22.
- Three convergence areas: automated machine-to-machine payments, tokenized financial assets, and
  emerging computing-capacity markets.
- Compute as an asset: "standardized digital contracts, essentially claims on compute resources",
  usable for financing and programmable settlement; agents select providers on price, performance and
  latency.
- Rights to GPUs could be represented as transferable digital contracts, used as collateral, or traded
  in real time through protocols such as MCP and A2A.
- Rails named explicitly: x402 and the Machine Payments Protocol (MPP).
- Caveat, stated by BlackRock: these markets are "largely speculative at this stage", and robust
  infrastructure for "permissions, identity, and compliance" must be built first.
- Number: hyperscaler cloud services revenue projected at roughly $1.1 trillion annually by 2030,
  about 29% CAGR from 2025. A projection, not a measurement.

## Conflict of interest to keep in view

BlackRock invests in AI infrastructure through the AI Infrastructure Partnership (with Microsoft,
Nvidia, MGX and GIP), which acquired Aligned Data Centers — reported at about $40bn, over 6.4 GW of
operational and planned capacity. A paper arguing that compute becomes a financeable asset is
therefore not disinterested. Read the mechanics, not the conclusion.
