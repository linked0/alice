## Structure (the article's description)

- **Legal**: each raise creates a Cayman **SPC** segregated portfolio under **Umia Launcher SPC**, via
  **MetaLeX's BORG** framework; IP, contracts and treasury in one entity.
- **Auction**: Uniswap v4 **CCA** (Continuous Clearing Auction), configurable, typically ~1 week;
  min/max raise range; clears progressively; **same price within a block**. Optional **early-bid phase**
  with **zkTLS** (Reclaim) eligibility proofs. Part of proceeds auto-creates a Uniswap v4 pool owned by
  the treasury; pool fees flow back to the treasury.
- **Treasury**: proceeds go to a treasury **contract**, not a team multisig. Team draws a monthly
  allowance fixed at entity creation. Larger spend, new issuance, comp changes, acquisitions, pivots
  and **liquidation** must pass a **decision market**.
- **Decision market**: proposer stakes and submits; conditional markets per outcome; participants trade
  on what the token should be worth **assuming** an outcome is implemented. A **No-Op** market
  represents the status quo; a proposal executes only if its **TWAP** exceeds No-Op by a preset
  threshold. Conditional markets are seeded with liquidity from the underlying spot pool.
- **Enforcement, two layers**: onchain (treasury moves impossible without decision-market approval) and
  offchain (Cayman entity ⇒ ignoring a decision is actionable in a real legal system).
- **Liquidation right**: holders can propose liquidation; if approved, remaining treasury is distributed
  **pro rata**. Umia calls it the "ultimate safeguard".

## $UMIA value capture (the article's account)

- **Curation**: future **Community Track** admission decided by decision markets paired with $UMIA.
- **Fees**: configurable fee switch on trading fees across spot markets and conditional AMMs; project
  creation fees not currently charged, can be added by governance. Fees can accrue in USDC and in
  project tokens.
- "Umia is the first venture built on Umia" — same SPC structure, decision markets and treasury rules.
- Largest single allocation reserved for the **public auction** ⇒ relatively high float at launch,
  against the low-float / high-FDV pattern; further issuance only via a **"Fluid Capital"** model
  approved by the market.
- Comparison drawn to **Cosmos/$ATOM**: ecosystem growth without a clear economic link to the token.

## Caveats the article states

- **Community Track not live**; first projects via a **Curated Track** Umia selects ⇒ not permissionless.
- Entering Umia SPC means depending on a common legal framework Umia controls.
- TWAP raises but does not eliminate manipulation cost; in a **thin** market a few participants can
  still move price, and because $UMIA-paired markets gate Community Track admission, **market depth
  affects which projects are admitted**.
- The author's earlier critique: a prediction market works when participants can predict but not
  **cause** an outcome; a decision market's trading **directly causes** the corporate decision.
