comparing those two sets.

## Cross-check performed

The official 25 manga works listed on ko.wikipedia were compared, title by title, against the
paste's ranks 1-25. All 25 match, allowing for title-form differences (SLAM DUNK/슬램덩크,
DEATH NOTE/데스노트, ONE PIECE/원피스, MONSTER/몬스터, HELLSING/헬싱, 아키라/AKIRA,
유☆유☆백서/유유백서). No work is in one set and not the other. Ranks 26-50 are therefore the
works that were voted for and did not make the official selection.

## Known defects in the paste

- No vote counts anywhere, so margins are unknown.
- The expert list is given only to joint 10th; whether it ran further is unknown.
- The paste is dated '2006년 발표' with no publisher named.
