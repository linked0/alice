## Claims (unverified here)

- Daily revenue **>$4M** two months after launch; roughly **2×** Hyperliquid and **5–6×** Tron and
  Solana; about **two-thirds of all Ethereum L2 revenue**.
- Robinhood Chain built for stock tokens; supported stock token issuance from **Day 1** alongside
  **Uniswap** and **Morpho**.
- **CashCat** drew early attention but followed the usual memecoin playbook.
- The novel step: launchpads such as **Pons** allowed memecoins to launch **paired with stock tokens**
  rather than ETH/SOL, **starting with LONG**. Projects airdropped stock tokens to memecoin holders.
- **Pons** at one point out-earned both Hyperliquid and pump.fun on daily revenue.
- The article's four reasons: stock tokens from Day 1; the Robinhood brand's retail/degen association
  (GameStop, options, meme stocks); existing users and liquidity; absorbing existing infrastructure
  (Uniswap, Morpho, EVM tooling).
- Closing question: whether memecoin liquidity converts into durable native projects.
