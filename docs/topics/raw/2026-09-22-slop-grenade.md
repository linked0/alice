# slop grenade — 2026-09-22

Claims and citations only. No article body is copied here; the point of this file is that the
sources can be reopened rather than re-remembered (see README.md).

## The phrase

- Tobi Lütke, CEO of Shopify, on **The Knowledge Project** podcast (Shane Parrish), Tuesday
  **17 September 2026**: "We call those 'slop grenades' that people toss at each other… That's
  definitely a bad thing."
- Also: "You don't really read it, and now it has to be reviewed by your colleagues, and they are
  like, 'this doesn't look right'."
- Reports differ on the venue — tech.co describes a CBS News interview, Fortune the podcast. Both
  are September 2026 and the wording is the same, so the phrase is attributed to Lütke here without
  pinning it to one appearance.

## The sibling term

- **workslop** is the material; a **slop grenade** is the act of throwing it at someone. The word
  comes from research by **BetterUp Labs** with **Stanford's Social Media Lab**.
- Survey of **962** American full-time desk workers, 2026: **52.7%** reported sending workslop to
  colleagues, and it was more common where the organisation encouraged AI use.
- tech.co separately cites 40% of AI work still requiring rework; it does not name the source for
  that figure, so treat it as unverified.
