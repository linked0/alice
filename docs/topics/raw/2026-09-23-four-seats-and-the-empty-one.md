# Source: Kakao Pay / Kakao Bank / Fireblocks MoU (2026-09-22)

jay pasted a Korean briefing on 2026-09-23 and asked the item to focus on the participants and
their roles.

## Verified from coverage

- Fireblocks signed an MoU with Kakao Pay and Kakao Bank to build digital-asset infrastructure for
  Korea; announced Monday 2026-09-22.
- Scope: explore stablecoin distribution frameworks suited to Korea's regulatory, security and
  service requirements, then run proof-of-concept tests.
- No launch, investment or implementation timeline was given.
- Fireblocks provides digital-asset infrastructure to more than 2,500 institutions, including over
  100 banks (company figure).
- Kakao Pay operates mobile payments and financial services; KakaoBank is one of Korea's largest
  internet-only banks.
- In July, Kakao Group signed a separate MoU with Circle to explore blockchain-based payment
  infrastructure and digital asset technology, including won-denominated stablecoins.
