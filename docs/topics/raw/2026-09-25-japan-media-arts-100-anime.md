## Cross-check performed

The 25 animation works listed on ko.wikipedia as the official selection were compared title by
title against the paste's ranks 1-25. All 25 match. Ranks 26-50 are therefore works that were
voted for and not selected.

## Known defects in the paste

- The 〈마운틴 헤드〉 line described above.
- 공동 8위 carries four titles, so the expert 'top ten' has eleven entries.
- No vote counts, so no margins.
- Some entries are franchises, not single works (용자 시리즈, 우주전함 야마토 시리즈,
  미소녀 전사 세일러 문 시리즈, 드래곤볼 애니메이션 시리즈, 도라에몽 극장판).
