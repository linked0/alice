## Status of the attribution

NOT THE GUARDIAN'S. The Guardian's own list is Robert McCrum, 'The 100 best novels written in
English', https://www.theguardian.com/books/2015/aug/17/the-100-best-novels-written-in-english-the-full-list
— fetched in full on 2026-09-25, 100 entries, chronological 1678-2000, English-language only,
no Tolstoy, no Flaubert, no Proust, no Beloved, no Han Kang. The real list is saved beside this
file as real-guardian-100.txt.

The 'only Asian writer' note is refuted by the pasted list itself: Ishiguro (24, 59), Rushdie
(23), Roy (32), Mistry (49), Naipaul (78).

Which body actually produced the pasted ranking could not be established. Not guessed at.
