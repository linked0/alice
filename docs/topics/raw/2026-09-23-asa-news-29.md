## Japan

- ~**40 banks** to pilot interbank remittance on **tokenized deposits**, led by **GMO Aozora Net Bank**
  with **DeCurret DCP** and **ABeam Consulting**; **Resona**, **Shoko Chukin**, **Bank of Yokohama** as
  observers.
- Target: low-cost instant settlement **24/7/365**, **without the Zengin System**.
- Tests **two approaches in parallel**: directly linking interbank deposit accounts, and stablecoins.
- Separately: **FSA + MoF + Bank of Japan** reviewing a blockchain concept for 24/7 instant settlement
  of **stocks and government bonds**, tokenizing part of **BoJ current account deposits**.
- FSA to seek eased procedures for **trust-type yen stablecoin transactions over ¥1 million** in its tax
  reform request; could open high-value payments (cars, real estate) from **FY2027**.

## Hong Kong

- **2026-08-24**: **Standard Chartered Hong Kong** becomes the **first bank distribution agent** for
  **HKDAP**, the regulated HKD stablecoin issued by **Anchorpoint**. Approved distributors now **five**.
- Initial use cases: tokenized money market fund subscription/settlement, intragroup treasury and
  liquidity, cross-border payments.
- **HSBC** reportedly preparing app-linked distribution ⇒ institutional vs app-linked split.
- **HashKey Exchange** offering Franklin Templeton's **grBENJI** tokenized MMF to professional investors.

## Korea

- **2026-08-26**: **Shinhan Financial Group** + **Visa** MoU — issuance, remittance, redemption on
  **Visa's stablecoin platform**; extends to card payment settlement.
- Same week: **Dunamu** + Visa partnership; reportedly reviewing **OUSD**.
- Newsletter's own comment: use-case validation moving ahead of issuer rules on Visa-operated
  infrastructure, "which leaves core payment rails dependent on external networks for now."
- **2026-12-03**: cross-border digital asset transfer businesses subject to **advance registration and
  Bank of Korea reporting**. DAXA briefing held on the dedicated FX reporting network.
- **2027-01-01**: digital asset taxation takes effect; fairness debate because **dollar FX gains are
  tax-exempt while stablecoin gains may be taxable**.
- Hanwha Investment & Securities pursuing domestic security tokens + overseas RWA-backed tokens.
- Mirae Asset (Hyeon-Joo Park): "Digital X" vision, KRW **20–30 trillion** gold coin, tokenizing large
  caps such as Samsung Electronics and SK Hynix.

## Singapore / other

- **DBS**: ~**S$10 billion** of tokenized payments over two years.
- **UOB + HSBC**: live cross-border HKD tokenized-deposit transaction on the **Swift ledger**; first
  Singapore-headquartered bank to do so.
- **Visa + Nium**: seven-day settlement tested under MAS **BLOOM**.
- **CIMB Islamic Bank**: settled ~**$342 million** of a **RM1.68 billion** sukuk with tokenized deposits
  — first Malaysian case combining tokenized deposits and Islamic bonds.
- **Osaka Prefecture**: JPY **28.89 million** subsidies to four pilots, three testing **JPYC** and
  **USDC** payments at stores and terminals.
- **Pakistan**: building a virtual asset regulatory framework with **$200,000**, ~8% of approved budget.
