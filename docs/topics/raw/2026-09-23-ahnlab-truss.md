## The product, as described

- **트러스 (TRUSS)**: enterprise digital-asset platform built **on-premise** — on the financial
  institution's own servers and internal network, not external cloud. Keys, approval policy and
  transaction data stay inside; the institution operates the platform itself.
- **Core infrastructure**: key management, approval policy, audit records, admin console, chain
  integration. Key management can use **MPC**, **HSM**, or the two combined.
- **Asset operations**:
  - **Security Vault** — the institution's own holdings, stored separately from operating systems,
    movable only through a pre-defined multi-approval process.
  - **Custody** — customer assets; transaction signers and the number of required approvers are
    configurable **to match the institution's existing internal approval rules (기존 결재 규정)**.
- **Expansion modules, activated by licence**: **Retail Wallet** (individual customers, under the
  institution's own brand), **Pay** (stablecoin payment and settlement), **Tokenization** (RWA and
  security-token issuance and operation).
- Existing **계정계 / 정보계** systems and service applications are **not replaced**; standard
  integration modules connect them. Supported chains can be added as the business expands.
- ABC states it applied its own MPC technology, a blockchain data indexer, monitoring, approval policy
  and failure-response standards.

## The limitation the report states plainly

- **Adopting TRUSS does not by itself allow a financial institution to conduct regulated business such
  as digital-asset custody.** Providing such services requires separate per-business licensing,
  including acceptance of a **VASP (가상자산사업자) filing**.

## Quote
