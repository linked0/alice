# Source: ESCB response to the Commission's MiCA review (2026-09-22)

jay pasted a Korean briefing on 2026-09-23. The submission itself was not read; this card is built
from coverage, and every claim below should be checked against the published text before quoting.

## Reported facts

- The ECB together with all 27 EU national central banks published a response to the European
  Commission's MiCA review on 2026-09-22.
- Ask: remove the requirement that significant stablecoins hold 60% of reserves as bank deposits.
- Replacement proposed: a minimum proportion of reserves in assets maturing within one to five
  working days.
- Stated reason: the rule creates a direct link between issuers and credit institutions. In a run,
  holders redeem, issuers withdraw deposits at speed, and banks face liquidity strain caused by
  crypto-market stress rather than by their own lending.
- Current rules: at least 30% of reserves as bank deposits; 60% for tokens designated "significant".
- Consultation runs to 2026-09-30. Current rules stand until the Commission acts.
- Also reported: a proposal to widen the existing ban on paying interest so that it covers indirect
  yield earned through crypto lending or staking.
