[Crypto/Issue] Mantle Q3 Recap: Powering Borderless Access to Global Capital Markets
Written by @ponyo_fp

DISCLOSURE, from the report itself: "The report is based on the independent research of the author
sponsored/funded by Mantle." The author may hold positions; Four Pillars may hold investments in
assets or protocols discussed; FP Validated may operate as a node in networks discussed.

--- THE REPORT'S CLAIMS AND FIGURES, as pasted ---

Key takeaways
- Q3 organised around the three barriers named in Mantle's 18 August brand refresh: geography,
  gatekeepers, market hours.
- Stablecoin supply on Mantle grew 8.6% to $572m; Paxos's USDG went live as a natively issued
  dollar and Mantle joined the Global Dollar Network.
- More than 1,300 tokenized stocks now available, up from four tokenized assets of any kind a year
  ago; SpaceX, Bending Spoons, Jersey Mike's and SHEIN listed within days of their IPOs; pre-IPO
  allocations opened through OpenStock.
- Bybit's wallets on Mantle reached $674m, the largest holder onchain; a new DeFi vault opened to
  any wallet.

Dashboards and totals
- Public Blockworks dashboard since 20 August. On 15 September it listed 1,327 tokenized assets on
  Mantle, up from 71 in January, with $344m in circulation, 71% higher than at the start of the year.
- L2Beat: $1.45bn secured on Mantle at quarter's end, +16.5% over three months; USDT0 the largest
  line at $445m, ahead of bridged ETH at $406m.

Beyond borders (geography)
- Stablecoin supply $572m (+8.6% over the quarter), of which USDT0 $458m — roughly 11% of all
  USDT0 in circulation. Remainder: Ethena's USDe, Ondo's USDY, USDC, Agora's AUSD.
- USDG (Global Dollar), Paxos's stablecoin, regulated in Singapore by MAS and in Europe under MiCA.
  Contract live on Mantle since 27 August. With the 3 September announcement Mantle became a network
  partner in the Global Dollar Network alongside more than 150 firms and roughly $3.5bn of USDG in
  circulation. Joshua Cheong, Mantle's Head of Business Development and Product, framed the point as
  fiat moving intraday on and off the network. Mantle points to its compliance stack, including
  Chainalysis KYT coverage, as part of what institutional issuers require.
- MNT on CCIP: the Super Portal, the bridge co-built with Bybit that carries MNT between Ethereum
  and Solana, migrated from LayerZero's OFT standard to Chainlink CCIP over a scheduled window of
  9–15 July, with Mantle configuring its own transfer controls under the CCT standard. Chainlink's
  Q2 review lists the migration; its token directory shows MNT pools live on Ethereum, Solana and
  HyperEVM.

Beyond gatekeepers
- xStocks listings: SpaceX 19 June, Franklin Templeton's US equity index 23 June, Bending Spoons
  7 July, Jersey Mike's 24 July, SHEIN 4 September — each within days of the underlying IPO or
  launch. Supply read directly from Mantle mainnet contracts: 30,000 SPCXx, 100,000 BSPx,
  152,356 JMKEx, 525,577 SHEINx.
- Blockworks counts 743 priced tokenized stocks on Mantle as of 15 September; rwa.xyz counts more
  than 730 xStocks contracts. A year ago the network carried four tokenized assets of any kind.
  Distribution runs through Fluxion, Merchant Moe and Bybit, which has supported xStocks deposits
  and withdrawals on Mantle since April.
- OpenStock, built by Polynomial, went live on Mantle 2 July with pre-IPO vaults: depositors put in
  USDT0 and hold a token for their share of an allocation, the underlying shares sit in a named,
  audited SPV, and the token redeems at net proceeds once the company lists. First vault was
  Zhongji Innolight, an optical-module supplier Polynomial describes as a $197bn company, ahead of
  its Hong Kong listing; by 30 July Mantle reported $9.3m into the vault within days, with nearly
  half of first-day deposits coming through Mantle.
- THE GAP, in the report's own words: Blockworks shows about $4.0bn of issuer-minted stock supply
  on the network against $4.5m in circulation, and rwa.xyz counts the same $4.5m in distributed
  hands with RWA holders on the network up 18% over the past 30 days to 3,973. "Availability is far
  ahead of demand, and both figures will move together as Bybit routes more of its xStocks flow
  on-chain."
- Bybit's own wallets on Mantle hold $674m across ETH, MNT and stablecoins, which makes the
  distribution partner the largest single holder on the chain.
- The Mantle Vault on Bybit, a CeFi product, drew its first $100m within a month of launch. A
  separate DeFi vault launched 25 August open to any wallet, with CIAN packaging the strategy,
  Grove allocating into Sky's Savings Rate and Fluxion providing the front end. Deposits are USDC
  and USDT0, the structure is non-custodial and unleveraged, and a 5.14m GROVE incentive program
  backs the launch. First time a Mantle yield product has been available without an exchange
  account.

Beyond market hours
- SHEINx listed in Hong Kong on 1 September and moved onto Fluxion's AMM on 4 September, trading
  through the hours the Hong Kong exchange is closed. Fluxion pairs its AMM with RFQ execution,
  which Mantle describes as atomic RFQ, so larger orders clear against quotes while pools carry the
  continuous market. Every xStock listing comes with a rewards layer — xPoints and a share of 1m
  Fluxion Points for trading, holding and providing liquidity.
- On 17 July Mantle reported $116.4m in tokenized active strategies running on the network, third
  across all chains on RWA Foundation data, and puts 96% of the RWA value on Mantle in
  yield-bearing assets. Blockworks' category view of the $344m in circulation: $149m in the MI4
  index fund, $141m in yield-bearing stablecoins from Ethena and Maple, $29m in treasuries, $21m in
  tokenized gold.
- InsightX, the prediction market built on Mantle, reported trading volume at a new high of $23m on
  7 July and passed $500m of total network volume in its first month on 15 July, with every
  prediction settling on Mantle in seconds.

Heading to Q4
- Mantle Key Advisor Emily Bao, who also runs spot at Bybit: "The future won't be defined by who
  tokenizes the most assets, but by who builds the markets around them."
- Robinhood Chain went live in June with stock tokens as its base asset. Once launchpads such as
  Pons and long.xyz let memecoins launch paired against those stock tokens, DEX volume went from
  between $3bn and $4.6bn a week through July and August to $11.4bn in the week of 31 August and
  $13.1bn the week after, with $132m of fees in a single week and Pons alone collecting $121m since
  mid-August. Chain TVL went from zero at the start of July to $930m.
- On Solana, where xStocks first listed, the tokens held $450m on 15 September, up 32% over the
  quarter and nearly six times the level of a year earlier. Tokenized equities now hold roughly
  $2.8bn, "and the two fastest-growing ones are the two where a market formed around the tokens."
- Mantle RWA Day at Token2049 Singapore on 6 October, with Franklin Templeton and Chainlink on the
  bill.

NOTES ON SOURCING (written when the card was built, 2026-09-23)

Reported: all of the above is the report's. I did not open the Blockworks dashboard, rwa.xyz,
L2Beat, the Chainlink directory, any Mantle mainnet contract, or Polynomial's OpenStock docs, and
checked no figure. Where the card repeats a number it is repeating Four Pillars repeating a
dashboard.

The disclosure is not a footnote here: this is a sponsored report about its sponsor. The card
states that at the top and then credits the report for publishing the $4.5m figure anyway.

The card's own arguments, labelled as such in the body:
 1. That the $4.0bn shelf against $4.5m in circulation — roughly 890 to 1 — is the finding, and
    that it proves Bao's own sentence in the least flattering way available: tokenizing an asset
    is not creating a market for it.
 2. That the report's own counterexample tells you what "building a market around them" has
    actually meant so far. What moved Robinhood Chain's volume was memecoins launching paired
    against stock tokens, not institutional distribution. The report reaches that data and does
    not draw the conclusion.
 3. That Bybit being the largest holder on the chain ($674m) makes the distribution partner also
    the balance sheet, so the growth thesis and the concentration risk are the same entity.
 4. That OpenStock's pre-IPO vault is the genuinely novel object in the quarter and the report does
    not discuss its failure modes: allocation not received, listing pulled or delayed, SPV
    enforceability, and no price discovery before the listing prices it.
 5. That three different dollars appear in one day's reading — a bank's (SoFiUSD), a regulated
    issuer's (USDG under MAS and MiCA), and a bridged one (USDT0 at 80% of Mantle's stablecoin
    supply) — and that the concentration in USDT0 is a risk the report does not weigh.
