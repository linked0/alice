# Source: SEC Innovation Exemption, and the first TSV timing (2026-09-17 / 09-22)

jay pasted a Korean briefing on 2026-09-23 and asked for the item to relate to Tech #51.

## Verified from coverage

- SEC issued the "Innovation Exemption" on 2026-09-17, creating Tokenized Securities Venues (TSV)
  with five years of relief from registering as a national securities exchange.
- Qualified platforms may trade tokenised US-listed stocks on public, permissionless blockchains
  using licensed AMMs and liquidity pools.
- The tokens must retain traditional shareholder rights, including dividends and voting.
- 2026-09-22: Taylor Lindman, chief counsel of the SEC Crypto Task Force, said the first venues
  could begin taking shape as soon as next quarter, and that firms may soon publish notices
  describing planned venues. Custody starts with the "boring rules".

## From jay's briefing, NOT confirmed against the order text

- An issuer objection right: if the issuer objects, the name cannot be traded.
- Caps on the number of names and on volume.
