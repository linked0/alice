## en
- **DeFi: compute break-even volatility next to the APY.** For every jayverse-defi pool, publish fees-earned versus divergence loss over the same window as a required metric alongside the advertised APY, not the APY alone.
- **Verex: book the LMSR subsidy as an expected loss, not revenue.** The market maker's subsidy is the same short-volatility position as an LP pool, so account for it explicitly as a funded cost of liquidity in market maker accounting.
- **Auditor: flag any APY quoted without its offsetting divergence-loss or subsidy cost.** Add it as a review check for DeFi pool designs and Verex's market maker, since the name "impermanent loss" already hides the real cost once.
