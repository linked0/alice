  • Verex/Game: label board indicators as rules, not predictions. Any moving-average or band drawn on a market board is a restatement of the price already shown — write the entry/exit/stop rule before showing it, and treat a widely watched level as a coordination point, not a signal.
  • DeFi: rebalance on funding rate and open interest, not derived price lines. A liquid-staking or LP strategy should key decisions off series nobody can compute from price alone — funding rate is money someone actually pays — rather than off an SMA or RSI of the same feed.
  • Auditor: require out-of-sample testing before any parameter ships. A discipline check for indicator-based rules — hit rate and average win/loss together, net of fees and funding, versus simply holding — belongs in the same review as any other backtest claim.
