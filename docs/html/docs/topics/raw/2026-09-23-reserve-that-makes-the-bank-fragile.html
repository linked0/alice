Reported facts

  • The ECB together with all 27 EU national central banks published a response to the European Commission's MiCA review on 2026-09-22.
  • Ask: remove the requirement that significant stablecoins hold 60% of reserves as bank deposits.
  • Replacement proposed: a minimum proportion of reserves in assets maturing within one to five working days.
  • Stated reason: the rule creates a direct link between issuers and credit institutions. In a run, holders redeem, issuers withdraw deposits at speed, and banks face liquidity strain caused by crypto-market stress rather than by their own lending.
  • Current rules: at least 30% of reserves as bank deposits; 60% for tokens designated "significant".
  • Consultation runs to 2026-09-30. Current rules stand until the Commission acts.
  • Also reported: a proposal to widen the existing ban on paying interest so that it covers indirect yield earned through crypto lending or staking.
