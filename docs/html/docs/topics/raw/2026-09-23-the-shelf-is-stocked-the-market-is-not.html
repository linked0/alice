Chainlink's Q2 review lists the migration; its token directory shows MNT pools live on Ethereum, Solana and HyperEVM.

First vault was Zhongji Innolight, an optical-module supplier Polynomial describes as a $197bn company, ahead of its Hong Kong listing; by 30 July Mantle reported $9.3m into the vault within days, with nearly half of first-day deposits coming through Mantle.
